# Trademarks

*Draft for the lawyer's review (open-source plan, Unit 0.4).*

The code in this repository is open source under the AGPL-3.0-or-later (see `LICENSE`). The license covers the code, not the name or the icon. This page says how you may use them.

## What the marks are

- **"Theme Camp App"**, the name of the hosted service at themecamp.app run by Kline Technology LLC.
- **The TCA icon**, the hosted service's logo and favicon.

Both say "this is the hosted service at themecamp.app." People trust it with their camps' information, so it matters that nobody mistakes another instance for it.

## You may

- Say your instance is **"based on Theme Camp App"** or "built with Theme Camp App's code," and link to this repository.
- Refer to Theme Camp App by name when talking about the project: in articles, talks, comparisons, issue reports and pull requests.
- Keep the copyright and license notices in the code, as the AGPL requires.

## Please don't

- Call your instance, fork or service "Theme Camp App," or give it a name that could be confused with it.
- Use the TCA icon, or something that looks like it, for your instance. The code ships a neutral default icon and a script that makes one from your initials.
- Use "themecamp" in a domain or address for your instance in a way that suggests it's the hosted service.
- Suggest that Kline Technology LLC runs, supports, endorses or is responsible for your instance.

If you run your own instance, set your own product name, icon, operator name, contact address and legal pages in its configuration (see `docs/self-hosting.md`). Your instance's users are your users: you're responsible for their data and for the terms they agree to.

## Burning Man

Theme Camp App is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or verified by Burning Man Project. Please don't use Burning Man Project's names or marks (such as "Burning Man," "Black Rock City," "Decompression" or "Playa Events") in your instance's name or branding. If your instance uses Burning Man's APIs or data, you need your own agreement with Burning Man and must follow its terms, including its required disclaimer.
