Theme Camp App is run by Kline Technology LLC ("we", "us"). These terms cover using it, whether you run a camp on it or were invited by one.

## What it is

Theme Camp App helps Burning Man theme camps organize: their people, RSVPs, Stewards Sale ticket requests, dues, guides and the rest. It's free, and the people who build it are committed to keeping it free for theme camp organizers for as long as they have the time and energy to run it. They may one day ask for optional donations to help cover costs. During the pilot, camps join by arrangement with us.

Theme Camp App isn't affiliated with, or endorsed by, Burning Man Project.

## Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email address, so keep that email account secure. Each camp decides who it invites.

## What camps agree to

A camp that uses Theme Camp App, and its organizers, agree to:

- use it to organize their camp, lawfully;
- invite only people who expect to hear from the camp;
- keep people's information to the camp's purposes, and not share it outside the camp;
- not upload anything they don't have the right to share, or that harasses or harms anyone;
- tell us promptly about anything that looks wrong, like someone seeing information they shouldn't.

We can suspend a camp that breaks these terms. We'll tell its Admins why.

## Your data

A camp's information belongs to the camp, and your own details are yours. You let us store and process them only to run Theme Camp App for the camp. How we handle them is in the Privacy Policy.

## The service

The pilot is offered as it is, with best-effort support and no guarantee of availability. Features will change, and we'll tell camps before we remove anything they rely on. We can't be responsible for losses from the service being unavailable or wrong. To the extent the law allows, our total liability is limited to what you've paid us, which during the pilot is nothing.

## Ending

A camp can stop using Theme Camp App at any time, and can ask for a full export of its data or for it to be deleted. If we end the pilot, or stop offering Theme Camp App, we'll give camps' Admins at least 30 days' notice and a full export first.

## Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we'll tell camps' Admins before the change takes effect.
