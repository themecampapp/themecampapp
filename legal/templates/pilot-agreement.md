- The camp's data belongs to the camp. We use it only to run the app for the camp.

## What the camp agrees to

- Use the app to organize the camp, and only invite people who expect to hear from it.
- Keep the camp's people's information to the camp's purposes.
- Tell us promptly if you think something's wrong, like someone seeing data they shouldn't.
