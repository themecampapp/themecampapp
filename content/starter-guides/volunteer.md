Our camp is built and run by its campers. **Everyone takes at least one camp job**, and you're welcome to take as many as you like. It's the best way to meet people and to make camp happen.

Sign up on the **[Volunteer page](/volunteer)**, which shows how many people each job needs and who's already signed up. Some jobs, like the ones the camp elects, are filled by Leads; the page lists those too. It always has the current list.

## Jobs many camps have

*Edit this list to match the jobs on your Volunteer page.*

- **Volunteer coordinator** (before the burn): fills open jobs, shares each job's duties, and makes sure teams know they're working together.
- **Logistics lead** (before the burn, and build week): point person for inventory, storage, build and strike.
- **Camp layout** (before the burn): plans where the camp's structures go.
- **Build crew** (build week): builds the camp before the gates open. See the [SAP guide](/guides/sap).
- **Kitchen and meals** (during the burn): plans shared meals and keeps the kitchen running.
- **Strike lead and crew** (the end of the burn): packs up camp.
- **MOOP lead** (the end of the burn): leads the final sweep for matter out of place, so we leave no trace.
