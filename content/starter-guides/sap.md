A **Setup Access Pass (SAP)** lets you arrive before the gates open to help build camp. Build week is one of the best parts of the burn, as we create a city from scratch. It's also hard physical work, often in the hot sun.

## How to ask for one

Ask for a SAP on your [RSVP](/rsvp), picking the day you'd like to arrive from the days the camp has passes for. Our leads decide who gets one. If you're approved, you'll get an email, and then another once your pass is ready to download from your home page.

## The rules for everyone on a SAP

- A SAP isn't a chance to party early. Expect to work with the build crew on camp infrastructure every day. We also allow time to set up your own space on your first day.
- Expect to stay in camp for most of early arrival. The days are long, and the crew counts on you.
- Put a realistic arrival day. If you're unsure, pick a later one.
- A pass is good from 12:01 am on the day it's issued for.
- **Print your pass.** Without the printed pass at the gate, you can't come in early.
