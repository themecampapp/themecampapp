Every camper who's coming pays camp dues. Dues cover what we share as a camp: the common spaces, kitchen, shade, power and whatever else we build together.

*Edit this: how your camp decides on dues each year.*

## How much, and how to pay

What you owe and how to pay are on your **[home page](/)** once the Treasurer has set them for the year. If the Treasurer has put you on a dues tier (like a need-based amount), your home page shows that tier's amount. Until then, the Dues card says they aren't set yet.

You owe dues once you've [RSVP'd](/rsvp) **Going** or **Leaning yes**.

## After you pay

The Treasurer records payments by hand, so it can take a few days for your home page to show **Paid**. If it still doesn't after a week or so, message the Treasurer with the date and how you paid.

## If plans change

If you've paid and then can't come, change your [RSVP](/rsvp) to *Not going*. The Treasurer is told automatically and will follow up with you.

## If dues are a hardship

Talk to the Treasurer or one of our leads. We're happy to work something out. Only the Treasurer can see anyone's dues.
