## Early arrival and build

A small crew arrives before the event opens to build camp: shade, the common spaces, and power. Early arrival needs a pass that the camp requests for the crew, so **say on your [RSVP](/rsvp)** if you'd like to come early, and our leads will confirm who's on the build crew.

## Your dwelling or vehicle

Describe what you're bringing on your RSVP (tent, Shiftpod, van, trailer, RV) and its **approximate footprint**, like *10 x 20 ft*. Leads use this to lay out camp, so accurate sizes really help.

## Generators and power

If you're bringing a generator, say so on your RSVP. Leads plan generator placement around the whole camp's needs. Plan some way to block the noise: sheets of plywood standing on all four sides (not the top), 2 to 3 feet away from the generator so it doesn't overheat, work well.

## Arrival and departure dates

Add your arrival and departure dates to your RSVP as soon as you know them, even rough ones. You can change them any time.

## Strike

Strike is the camp's pack-up at the end of the burn. **Everyone helps**, and we leave no trace. Plan your departure so you can take part, and tell a lead early if you'll need to leave before strike.
