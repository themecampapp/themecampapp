We're a theme camp at Burning Man: a group of people who build a small part of Black Rock City together every year, and share it with everyone who comes by.

## What happens in camp

*Edit this list to match your camp.*

- **Our offering:** what we share with the city (a bar, a workshop, art, a stage, shade, food).
- **Community:** meals together, sunset hangs, and heading out onto the playa as a group.
- **Building it together:** everyone helps build, run and pack up camp.

## Camping with us

Camping with us means being part of a team: we build camp together, share the work while we're there, and strike it together at the end. Every camper chips in on dues and takes on a camp job.
