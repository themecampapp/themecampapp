Our camp gets access to Burning Man's **Stewards Sale**, which lets some of our campers buy tickets before the main sale.

## How it works

1. **RSVP for the year.** Only campers who are *Going* or *Leaning yes* can request tickets.
2. **Request what you need** on the [Stewards Sale tickets](/tickets/request) page: how many tickets (one can be for a partner you're camping with), and whether you need a **vehicle pass**. If you already have a ticket from somewhere else, tick that box so we don't count you.
3. **Burning Man emails you** with how to complete your purchase, once the camp has sent in its list.

## Good to know

- The email on your Burning Man (Burner) profile must be the same as the one you use here. Burning Man sends Stewards Sale tickets only to your Burner profile email.
- If your plans change, update your [RSVP](/rsvp) right away so your ticket can go to someone else.

*Our leads share this year's Stewards Sale dates as soon as Burning Man announces them.*
