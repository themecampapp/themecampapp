Only the camp's Leads and Admins see this guide. It covers the Lead pages and the jobs that come up over the season. Some sections are about features your camp may have turned off (Admin → Features); skip those.

## Your tools

As a Lead you get an extra menu under the header:

- **[Roster](/roster):** everyone in this season's camp in one list. Search by name, playa name or email; filter by RSVP, ticket request, SAP and volunteering; sort by arrival or years camped; and pick the columns. Ready-made views answer the usual questions (No RSVP, No ticket request, Arrivals, SAP to decide, Generators, No volunteer role). Click someone to see their season beside the list, and step through the list with Previous and Next. Tick people to **Nudge picked to RSVP** (you'll see who gets it, and who's skipped, before anything is sent). **Copy emails** copies the list you're looking at, and **Export this view** downloads it.
- **[Tickets](/tickets):** the Stewards Sale list to send to Burning Man.
- **[People](/people):** everyone in the app, with search and filters. Open a person to edit them. **Export CSV** downloads everyone.
- **[Inquiries](/inquiries):** people who asked to join through the Join page.
- **[Queue](/queue):** problems the app couldn't sort out by itself.
- **[Announcements](/announcements)** and **[Dates](/dates):** what members see on their home page.
- **[Guide editor](/guide-editor):** the camp's guides, this one included.


Your home page opens on **Overview**: what the camp needs now (missing RSVPs and ticket requests, SAPs to decide, new inquiries, the Queue, open spots in jobs), each linking straight to the list. **My participation** has your own RSVP, ticket request and dues; a number on it means you have something to do.

## The season at a glance

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Right after the burn | An Admin rolls the app over to next year, recording who came. Follow up with newcomers who asked to join. |
| Fall | Invite returning campers. Work through new inquiries. Keep the Queue empty. |
| Winter | Stewards Sale: share the dates, get everyone's ticket request in, lock requests, send the list to Burning Man. |
| Spring | The Treasurer sets dues and follows up on unpaid dues. Chase missing RSVPs. |
| Summer | Logistics: arrival dates, SAPs for the build crew, generators and dwellings. Final headcount. |

## Inviting campers

- **Returning campers, in bulk:** People → **Invite returning campers** lists everyone who came last year, has an email nobody else uses, and hasn't been invited yet.
- **One person:** open them in People (or **Add person** if they're new) and press **Send invite**.
- **What they get:** an email asking them to sign in with that address. They then ask for a sign-in link, which works once and lasts 15 minutes. If a link "didn't work", it usually expired: have them ask for a new one. Admins can see each step under **Admin → Sign-in log**.
- **Wrong email?** Before they've signed in, fix it on their person page (a pending invite is re-sent). After, they change it on their profile; if they signed in with someone else's address, use **Unlink login**, fix the email, and invite again.

## Newcomers (Inquiries)

1. **Assign** each inquiry to a Lead and move it along: **New → Contacted → Met → Invited** or **Declined**. Add a dated note after each conversation.
2. **Invite** when they're ready. If their email matches someone already in the app, you'll be asked whether it's the same person.
3. Don't write anything about health or other private history in notes.

## RSVPs

- The Roster's **No RSVP** view lists invited campers who haven't answered. **Send RSVP nudge email** reminds each of them; anyone nudged in the last 3 days is skipped.
- To enter someone's RSVP for them, open them in People and **edit** this year's RSVP. They see who changed it and when.
- Switching someone to *Not going* withdraws their ticket request and flags any dues they've paid for the Treasurer.

## Stewards Sale tickets

1. **Share the dates** once Burning Man announces them, and point people to the [Tickets guide](/guides/tickets-stewards-sale).
2. **Get everyone's answer.** [Tickets](/tickets) shows who's buying through the camp, who has a ticket elsewhere, and who hasn't answered. **Record for them** if someone tells you directly.
3. **Check emails.** Burning Man sends to each camper's Burner profile email, so the email in the app has to match.
4. **Lock requests** while you finalize. **Copy list** copies a table that pastes into a spreadsheet for Burning Man. **Unlock** afterwards for late changes.

## Setup Access Passes (SAPs)

1. An Admin sets this season's SAP days and adds the passes Burning Man allocates, with their PDFs, under **Admin → SAPs**.
2. Leads decide on the [SAPs](/saps) page: pick a pass for each request and **Approve**, or **Don't approve**.
3. The camper is emailed when approved and again when their PDF is ready, and must print it for the gate. If their plans change, the pass goes back into stock.

## Dues

Who has paid is private to the **Treasurer**. They set the year's standard amount and payment instructions on the Dues page and mark each camper Unpaid, Paid or Waived. For sliding-scale dues they can add **tiers** (for example *Low income* or *Kitchen lead*, each with its own amount) and put campers on them; only the Treasurer and the camper see who is on which tier, and tiers carry over to the next year. Point anyone who finds dues a hardship to the Treasurer.

## After the burn: rolling over

An Admin does this once, from **Admin → Season**: tick who actually came, then **Roll over**. Everyone's RSVP, ticket request, SAP request and dues start blank for the new year, and nothing is deleted. **Switch season** undoes it.

## Roles and people who leave

- Roles (Lead, Treasurer, Admin and any your camp has made) are given by an Admin, on each person's page. Ask one of your camp's Admins if someone needs a role.
- A job, even an elected one like Camp lead, isn't access: an Admin makes its holder a Lead separately if they need the Lead pages.
- **Deactivate** someone who has left: they're signed out and drop out of every list, and their history is kept. **Reactivate** brings them back.
- Every change is in the person's **History**; Admins see everything under **Admin → Activity**.

## Privacy

Lead pages show things members never see: phone numbers, notes and everyone's contact details.

- Don't forward screenshots of Lead pages.
- **Export CSV** contains everyone's details, and each export is logged. Keep the file to yourself and delete it when you're done.
- Never record health information anywhere in the app.

## Getting help

If something looks broken or email isn't arriving, an Admin can grant {product} support read-only access under **Admin → Support access**, and every page support opens is listed in the camp's Activity.
