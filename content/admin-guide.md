Admins can do everything Leads and the Treasurer can, plus the **Admin** pages: the camp's settings and look, which features it uses, the wording members see, roles, seasons, and support. This guide covers those. For the day-to-day work of running the season (inviting people, the roster, tickets, announcements) see your camp's **Lead handbook** under Guides.

It's the same for every camp and kept up to date with the app, so it always describes what's actually there.

## Getting started

- **The pilot agreement.** A camp set up by {product} can't be used until one of its Admins accepts the [pilot agreement](/admin/agreement). It's short: read it once, accept it for the camp, and the rest of the app opens.
- **The setup checklist.** Your home page's Overview shows what's left to set up: the contact email, features, what your Leads can see, dues, a key date, a published guide, your people, a first invite, and the {product} Discord. Each item ticks itself off. Leads and Admins can hide it; an Admin can bring it back from [Admin → Season](/admin).
- **The tour.** Leads and Admins get a short guided tour of the Lead tools the first time. Take it again any time from **Take the tour** on your home page.
- **Help.** The [{product} Discord]({discord}) is the way to talk with the app's developer, and the best way to get support or ask for features.

## Your camp's look and details

[Admin → Settings](/admin/settings):

- **Camp name** and **logo** (JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC, up to 2 MB; it's shown in the header and on the sign-in page). Without a logo, the header shows your camp's initial.
- **Accent colour**, for buttons and highlights. It has to be light enough for dark text to read on it; darker colours are refused.
- **Contact email**: when someone replies to an email the app sent for your camp, the reply goes here. Without one, replies don't reach your camp.
- **Who hears about new inquiries** from the join page.
- **The most tickets a camper can request** in the Stewards Sale.
- **Dues payment methods**: the choices the Treasurer picks from when recording a payment (Venmo, Zelle, cash, or your own).

## Features

[Admin → Features](/admin/features) switches the optional parts of the app on and off:

- **Dues**: the Treasurer records who has paid (the app never takes payments).
- **Join page and inquiries**: a public page where people can say they're interested, and the Leads' pipeline.
- **Yearbook and directory**: photo cards for this year and past years, and the contact directory.
- **Setup Access Passes**: early-arrival requests, Lead decisions, and the passes Burning Man allocates.
- **Jobs**: the camp's jobs, from Camp lead to Kitchen. Members sign up for some; Leads fill others, like the ones the camp elects. Choose which jobs show in the Yearbook's Who's who.
- **Mailing addresses**: campers can add a postal address on their profile. Only Leads and Admins see it.

Switching a feature off hides its pages, menu links, questions and emails. **Nothing is deleted**: switch it back on and everything is where it was. Stewards Sale ticket requests and announcements are always on.

## The words members see

- **[Page wording](/admin/wording)**: the instructions on members' pages, like the join page's introduction, the RSVP and ticket pages, volunteer instructions and photo guidance. Write them your camp's way. Plain text, with full `https://` links, and `{camp}` and `{year}` filled in for you. **Reset** goes back to the default. What the app says about privacy, errors and status always stays as it is.
- **[Emails](/admin/emails)**: the invite, RSVP nudge, join confirmation and the other emails the app sends. Edit one to change it from then on; you can always reset it. The sign-in link email and the Leads' notification about new inquiries can't be changed.

## Roles and people

- **Only Admins give people roles**, on each person's page under People (**Add a role**). Someone with more than one role can do what any of them allows.
- **[Roles](/admin/roles)** shows what each role can do. A role is a level in each area of the camp's tools (People, Private details & notes, Roster & RSVPs, Tickets, Dues, SAPs, Jobs, Inquiries, Announcements & dates, Guides): **No access**, **View** or **Manage**.
- **Lead** and **Treasurer** start the way the app sets them up: Leads run the season, and the Treasurer handles dues. Change what either can do (for example, let Leads see who has paid without recording payments), and **Reset to the defaults** any time.
- **Custom roles** are for jobs that need some of the tools but not all: a tickets coordinator, or a registrar who handles inquiries and invites without seeing anyone's private details. **New role** starts from Lead, Treasurer, a ready-made one, or nothing. Archive a role you no longer use; whoever holds it loses it.
- **Private details & notes** covers phone numbers, emails and home locations people haven't shared in the directory, mailing addresses, and the notes kept on people. Give it only to roles that need it.
- **Admin** can't be changed: Admins can do everything. **Keep Admins to one or two people**, and have at least two so the camp isn't stuck if one is away. The app won't remove the last Admin.
- **Jobs** ([Manage jobs](/volunteers), when the feature is on) aren't access, even elected ones like Camp lead. A role is what someone can do in the app; a job is what they do for the camp. Next to anyone in a job who isn't a Lead, Admins see **Make Lead**, for camps whose job holders run the camp.
- Someone who's left: **Deactivate** them on their page. They're signed out and drop out of every list, and their history is kept. **Reactivate** brings them back. Campers can also leave the camp themselves from their profile.

## Seasons

[Admin → Season](/admin):

- **Rolling over**, once a year after the burn: tick who actually came, then **Roll over**. Everyone's RSVP, ticket request, SAP request and dues start fresh for the new year, and nothing is deleted. Dues tiers carry over for the Treasurer to adjust.
- **Switch season** goes back to an earlier season if you rolled over by mistake. Recorded attendance stays.
- **Past seasons** fill in your camp's history, back to 1996: add a year, then tick who camped that year. It shows in the Yearbook and on each person's page. A past season can be removed only while nobody has a record in it.

## Setup Access Passes

When SAPs are on, [Admin → SAPs](/admin/saps) is where you set the days campers can pick for early arrival this season, and add the passes Burning Man allocates (their serial numbers and PDFs). Leads then approve requests and assign passes on the SAPs page, and campers are emailed when their pass is ready to print.

## Keeping an eye on things

- **[Activity](/admin/activity)**: every recorded change in the app, newest first, and who made it. It can't be edited or deleted. Each person's own changes are also in the History on their page.
- **[Sign-in log](/admin/logs)**: every step of signing in, for when someone says they can't. The usual answers: sign-in links work once and last 15 minutes, so ask for a new one; check the spam folder; make sure they're using the email the camp has for them. Kept for 90 days; links themselves are never recorded.
- **Email limits.** Each camp can send a limited number of emails a day, and a camp whose emails bounce a lot or get marked as spam is paused until it's looked into. If emails seem stuck, ask on Discord.

## Support and your camp's data

- **Support access**: if something looks wrong, an Admin can let {product} support see the camp, read-only, for an hour, a day, 3 days or 7 days, at [Admin → Support access](/admin/support). Support can't change anything. You'll see a banner while it lasts, every page support opens is listed in Activity, and Admins are emailed when it starts and ends. End it any time.
- **Full export**: ask on Discord or by email for a full export of the camp (every record as spreadsheets, plus photos, pass PDFs and the logo). You'll get an email with a link to confirm; the zip then appears at [Admin → Exports](/admin/exports) for 7 days. For everyday lists, use the exports on each page, like People → Export.
- **Deleting the camp**: ask the same way. The camp closes at once and can be restored for 30 days, then it's gone for good.
- **Privacy**: {product} uses your camp's data only to run the app for your camp, and never sells or shares it. Every camp is kept apart from every other by the database itself. See the [Privacy Policy]({site}/legal/privacy) and [how we handle data]({site}/legal/data-handling).
